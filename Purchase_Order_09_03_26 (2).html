When is a Purchase Order Required?

A Purchase Order establishes a legally binding agreement that commits Columbia University to pay for goods and/or services upon successful delivery and completion from suppliers. This guide helps determine PO Documentation requirements, bidding thresholds, and workflow rules under updated Uniform Guidance

How many quotes do I need?

Competitive sourcing scales with the dollar amount — but the rules diverge by funding source. Check your project number to know which card applies: non-sponsored projects begin with UR, GT, or EN, while sponsored projects (grants) begin with GG or PG.

Non-sponsored funds

UR - GT - EN
Up to $15,000
1 quote / proposal
Price must be fair and reasonable. Seek competition where practical.
$15,001 – $350,000
Minimum 2 written quotes
Or a sole/single-source justification in CU Marketplace if non-competitive.
$350,001 and over
Minimum 3 written quotes
Formal RFP or sealed-bid process. Coordinate with Purchasing.

Sponsored funds

GG - PG
Up to $15,000
1 quote / proposal
Micro-purchase threshold. Price must be fair and reasonable.
$15,001 – $350,000
Multiple written quotes
From an adequate number of qualified sources — no fixed count. Retain the price analysis.
$350,001 and over
Formal competitive process
Sealed bid or competitive proposal from adequate qualified sources. Draft the Scope of Work and Bid Prep Form, then consult Purchasing.
CU Marketplace
Types of Purchase Orders
Catalog PO (UwPA) < $50k Streamlined
Issued for in-scope catalog items from suppliers with University-Wide Purchasing Agreements (UwPAs). Only 1 quote is needed regardless of dollar amount due to pre-negotiated pricing. Purchases under $50,000 bypass individual purchasing pre-approvals.
Non-Catalog PO Approval Req.
Used for goods and services outside catalog limits or non-UwPA vendors. Requires Central Purchasing review and competitive bidding if exceeding standard Uniform Guidance thresholds ($15,000).
Workflow Roles
Roles & Responsibilities in CU Marketplace
Role Primary Responsibilities
Shopper Creates shopping carts in CU Marketplace; assigns cart to a Procurement Initiator.
Procurement Initiator Performs quality checks, enters chartstrings, submits requisitions into workflow, and confirms receiving upon delivery.
Department Approver Reviews/approves requisitions; verifies alignment with policies, budget, and proper chartstrings.
Special / Ad Hoc Approver Inserted manually or triggered by commodity (e.g., radioactive materials, animals, capital equipment, lasers) or specific funding rules.
Central Purchasing Reviews catalog orders >$50k, non-catalog goods >$500, and non-catalog services. Approves and converts requisitions to POs.
